    Introducing Aurivane

    Practical AI for the moments customers reach out.

    Most enquiries arrive when your team is busy, on the phone, or off for the night. Aurivane puts a calm, accurate first response on your website — then hands the work to the right person with everything they need.

    • Answers grounded in your approved business content
    • Clear disclosure that visitors are talking to an assistant
    • Human handoff for anything sensitive, unusual or urgent
    • Built on a secure server-side design — keys never reach the browser
